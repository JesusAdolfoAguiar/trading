DAILY SESSION JOURNAL (Split Session Protocol)

Phase 1: The Personal Check-In (Pre-Session Readiness)
Before you even look at a chart, audit your mental capital. 
Operator Checks:
- No bandwidth (sleep/stress/baggage)? -> Screen off. No trade.
- Do you wanna be right, or be profitable? -> Accept variance.
- If you can walk away with profits, you can do so with losses.

Phase 2: Establishing Market Conditions (Macro & H1 View)
Define the environment (Trending, Ranging, or No Man's Land) to locate the core trade ideas for the session.
Chart Checks:
- What is the HTF Bias / True Draw on Liquidity? (Are we sweeping recent intraday pools, or expanding toward a massive Daily/Weekly target?)
- Move Length / ATR Check: Has the market already moved 400-500 pips directionally? (If yes, momentum is exhausted. Do not chase continuation).
- What is the H1 Trap? (Are we sweeping an Asia/London high/low, or retracing into an HTF FVG?)
- Is the target clear? (Aiming for 200-400 pips to the next major swing high/low).

TRADE 1 (Session A)

1. PRE-TRADE: (Approaching POI / M5/M1 Execution)
IL Checks (Execution & Waiting):
- Active Waiting: Is price in the void? Set an alert 5-10 pips away. Hands off the mouse.
- Trade the reaction: Wait for price to reach the H1 FVG / Sweep zone. No blind limits on H1.
- Let LTF prove it: Do I see M5/M1 wicks, failed displacements, or micro-MSS at the zone before clicking?
- Risk defined: Stop Loss strictly set at 50 pips. (Max 2 bullets per sub-session).
Notes: 

2. IN-TRADE: (Hold / Timeframe Shift / Managing Winner)
IL Checks (Management):
- Timeframe Shift Rule: Is the stop safe? YES -> CLOSE the M1/M5 chart. Manage the hold strictly from M15/H1.
- Follow structure, not ego: Target the major session liquidity (200-400 pips). Don't exit early just because of M5 noise.
- Re-entry protocol: If stopped out (50 pips) but the H1 idea is still fully valid, wait for the next M1/M5 exhaustion setup to re-enter.
Notes: 

3. POST-TRADE & CIRCUIT BREAKER: (Invalidation / Exit Sweet Spot)
IL Checks (Adaptation):
- The Exit Sweet Spot: Did price strike the opposing HTF Target POI? YES -> Zoom back to M1/M5 and exit on the first opposite exhaustion/MSS.
- Stubbornness is not discipline: Did the H1 structure break against you? If the H1 idea is invalidated, stop executing. Accept the loss.
