# The Balanced Circuit Breaker Protocol

Your brain cannot think its way out of an amygdala hijack (adrenaline/cortisol spike). You must force a physical reset to pull blood flow back to the prefrontal cortex (logic center).

**The Golden Rule:** The exact second the trigger hits, both hands leave the desk. 

### LEVEL 1: The "Missed Setup" (Hesitation / Boredom)
* **Trigger:** You hesitated and missed the A+ entry, OR you're watching a choppy range and feeling the urge to force a trade.
* **The Action (1 Minute):** Stand up, step one yard away from the desk, and look out the window at the horizon.
* **The Mechanic:** Taking hands off the desk breaks the physical connection. Shifting eyes to the horizon breaks chart tunnel-vision.
* **The Logic Injection:** Say aloud, *"The trade is gone. Do I want to be right, or be profitable?"*

### LEVEL 2: The "Post-Loss" Adrenaline Breaker (Revenge / Urge to Meddle)
* **Trigger:** Your Stop Loss just got hit, OR you feel the immediate, burning urge to re-enter (buy the rip).
* **The Action (2 Minutes):** Drop to the floor. Do 10-15 pushups (or 20 air squats).
* **The Mechanic:** Burns the cortisol and adrenaline spike. A violent pattern interrupt. By the time you sit back down, the heart rate is from exercise, not market anger.
* **The Logic Injection:** Sit back down and read, *"Focus on the current candle, not the last trade."*

### LEVEL 3: The "Mammalian Dive" (Full Tilt / 2 Consecutive Losses)
* **Trigger:** You took two losses in a row, OR you realize you forced a C-Game trade and closed it. You are on the verge of a spiral.
* **The Action (3-5 Minutes):** Walk to the bathroom and splash freezing cold water on your face.
* **The Mechanic:** Triggers the mammalian dive reflex. Cold water on the face physically forces your heart rate to slow down and shifts the nervous system out of "fight or flight".
* **The Logic Injection:** *"One is too much, one thousand are not enough. Edge is gone, screen is off."*
