PRE TRADE
Phase 1: The Personal Check-In (Pre-Session Readiness)
Before you even look at a chart, you must audit your mental capital. Are you ready for war, or are you bringing baggage into the session?
No bandwidth, no trade.
Focus on the current candle, not the last trade.
Do you wanna be right, or be profitable?
If you can walk away with profits, you can do so with losses.
Phase 2: Establishing Market Conditions (H1 Macro View)
You are looking at the chart. The goal is to define the environment without predicting it.
We look for the most recent M5 clean FVG to be filled.
No volume, no setup. Too much volume, no setup. Respect the cycle.
Am I seeing a setup, or just forcing my bias? Trade the live structure.

Phase 3: Approaching the POI (The Patience Game)
Price is nearing your H1/M15 zone. FOMO starts creeping in. You want to enter early to get a better price (Impulse Entry).
Zones are references, not walls. Trade the reaction.
Don't front-run the reversal. Let the LTF prove it.
Phase 4: LTF Confirmation (The Validation)
Price is inside the zone. You are down on the M1/M5 waiting for displacement and exhaustion. Your brain wants to see a pattern even if the context is wrong.
Wait for the shift, not the shape.
Would I take this in a backtest? If no, skip.

IN TRADE
Phase 1: The Initial Hold (Anxiety & PnL Watching)
This happens right after entry. The trade is floating, PnL is flickering, and your brain wants to panic at the first normal pullback.
Am I managing the chart, or managing my anxiety?
Focus on the structure, not the PnL.
Phase 2: The Urge to Meddle (Fear of Losing Profit)
The trade is in profit, but starts to retrace. Your hand hovers over the 'Close' button to secure the bag before it vanishes.
Has structure actually changed, or am I just scared of losing profit?
Structure is intact. Hands off the mouse.
Fear is not an exit signal.
Phase 3: Managing the Winner (Securing the Target)
The trade is moving beautifully, but FOMO/impatience is urging you to take profits before your technical exhaustion rules are met.
Let the LTF close the trade.
Phase 4: The Invalidation (Market Shifts & Ego Traps)
The trade has actually failed. The M5 displacement went against you. Now, your ego wants to hold on, hope, or ignore the stop loss.
Stubbornness is not discipline. Follow the structure, not the ego.
New data, new direction. Discipline is adapting.
