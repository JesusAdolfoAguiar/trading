# DAILY SESSION JOURNAL

## Phase 1: The Personal Check-In (Pre-Session Readiness)
Before you even look at a chart, audit your mental capital. 
**Operator Checks:**
- [ ] *No bandwidth (sleep/stress/baggage)?* -> Screen off. No trade.
- [ ] *Do you wanna be right, or be profitable?* -> Accept variance.
- [ ] *If you can walk away with profits, you can do so with losses.*

## Phase 2: Establishing Market Conditions (H1 Macro View)
Define the environment without predicting it. We look for the most recent M5 clean FVG to be filled.
**Chart Checks:**
- [ ] *No volume (overlapping M5 wicks)?* -> No setup. 
- [ ] *Too much volume (news spikes blowing past grid)?* -> No setup.
- [ ] *Am I forcing my bias?* -> Is the swing past 7-9 M5 candles? If yes, cycle is dead.
