DAILY SESSION JOURNAL (8 AM - 12 PM EST)

Phase 1: The Personal Check-In (Pre-Session Readiness)
Before you even look at a chart, audit your mental capital. 
Operator Checks:
- No bandwidth (sleep/stress/baggage)? -> Screen off. No trade.
- Do you wanna be right, or be profitable? -> Accept variance.
- If you can walk away with profits, you can do so with losses.

Phase 2: Establishing Market Conditions (Macro & H1 View)
Define the environment (Trending or Ranging) to locate the 1-2 core trade ideas for the session.
Chart Checks:
- What is the HTF Bias (Daily/H4)?
- What is the H1 Trap? (Are we sweeping an Asia/London high/low, or retracing into an HTF FVG?)
- Is the target clear? (Aiming for 200-400 pips to the next major swing high/low).

1. PRE-TRADE: (Approaching H1 POI / M5/M1 Execution)
IL Checks (Execution):
- Trade the reaction: Wait for price to reach the H1 FVG / Sweep zone. No blind limits on H1.
- Let LTF prove it: Wait for price to hit the $11.5 / $5.75 execution zones.
- Look for Exhaustion: Do I see M5/M1 wicks, failed displacements, or micro-MSS at the zone before clicking?
- Risk defined: Stop Loss strictly set at 50 pips. (Expect potential re-entries if stopped out early on a valid idea).

2. IN-TRADE: (Hold / Urge to Meddle / Managing Winner)
IL Checks (Management):
- Follow structure, not ego: Target the major session liquidity (200-400 pips). Don't exit early just because of M5 noise.
- Scared of losing profit? Did the M5/M1 structure actually break against the H1 idea? (No = Hold).
- Re-entry protocol: If stopped out (50 pips) but the H1 idea is still fully valid, wait for the next M1/M5 exhaustion setup to re-enter.

3. POST-TRADE & CIRCUIT BREAKER: (Invalidation / Ego Traps)
IL Checks (Adaptation):
- Stubbornness is not discipline: Did the H1 structure break against you? If the H1 idea is invalidated, stop executing. Accept the loss.
- Max Risk: How many executions did I take on the 1-2 ideas today? Did I overtrade?

Did I hit a trigger? (SL hit, missed entry, tilt?)
- No. A-Game execution.
- YES -> Which Physical Action did I just execute?
      - Lvl 1: Stood up / Window (Hesitation)
      - Lvl 2: 10-15 Pushups (Loss/Revenge urge)
      - Lvl 3: Splash Cold Water (Tilt / Max Executions Reached)
