# Trading Strategy Reset: Top-Down Fractal Approach

## Core Philosophy
- **Hard Reset**: We are completely rebranding the strategy and retraining our approach.
- **Fractals of Price**: The market moves in fractals. We must align with the larger structural narrative before executing on the micro scale.

## Analysis (The "What" and "Where")
- **Top-Down Analysis**: Analysis must strictly come from the Higher Time Frames (HTF). 
- **Directional Bias**: Use **Daily, H4, and H1** candles to determine the overarching trend, narrative, and direction of the move.
- The HTF dictates the "play" we are looking for.

## Execution (The "When")
- **Lower Time Frame (LTF) Role**: **M5 and M1** candles are now strictly for execution timing (entries and exits). They provide information for pulling the trigger, *not* for generating the analysis.
- **The Setup**: We are executing based on the M5 Fair Value Gap (FVG), bridging the gap up to the HTF FVG.
